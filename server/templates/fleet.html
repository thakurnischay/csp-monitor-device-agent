{{ ico('info', 14) }}How statuses are decided

Online = reported within the last {{ window }} minutes; Stale = reported within the last hour but not recently; Offline = longer than that, or never.

Device cells show a root-cause diagnosis (agent 2.0+): Software issue, Driver issue, Suspected hardware failure and Service unavailable are diagnosed faults; Degraded works with a limitation (for example low paper); Diagnosis required means the evidence is not enough yet. The coloured tag (Critical / High / Medium / Low) is the business severity; open a CSP for the evidence, affected workflows and recommended steps.

Unverified (amber) = the device is plugged in, but the agent could not confirm it is the right device (a generic entry such as "USB Input Device" was picked) - it is not shown as OK until the CSP uses Find my device on the agent page. Disconnected under a device means the CSP's PC is offline, so the device's current status can't be checked. A dash (–) means that CSP's agent is too old to report that device.
