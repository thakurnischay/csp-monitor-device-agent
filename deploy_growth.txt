Deploy: Device Sales (the CSP Growth Platform, merged into the dashboard at /growth)
====================================================================================
Adds the Device Sales section and the public order page to the live dashboard and moves the Growth Platform's data
(orders, email history, 535 CSPs) onto the server. Drip emails stay DRY - nothing is sent to CSPs by this deploy.
No change to the dashboard database or to the agents.

Like the Hindi deploy, it ADDS to the live files (other changes on the server are kept), tests every page in a
throwaway container first, and changes nothing if anything fails.

--- BEFORE YOU START -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Do NOT start the old Growth Platform (python run.py) on the laptop again after this. From now on the server is the
only copy of the orders and email history; two running copies would split the data.

--- STEP 1. On YOUR PC (PowerShell) - package and copy 5 files to the server --------------------------------------
cd C:\Users\ASUS\Downloads\csp-device-monitor
tar -czf growth_platform.tgz -C server --exclude=__pycache__ --exclude=growth_platform/data growth_platform
Get-Content "..\Product growth (2)\Product growth\csp-growth-platform\.env" | Select-String "^ORDER_PAGE_SHEET_MIRROR_URL=" | ForEach-Object { $_.Line } | Set-Content -Encoding ascii extra.env
ssh -p 2222 kapil@indev.eko.in "mkdir -p ~/growth"
scp -P 2222 deploy_growth.sh growth_platform.tgz extra.env "..\Product growth (2)\Product growth\csp-growth-platform\backend\data\state.json" "..\Product growth (2)\Product growth\csp-growth-platform\backend\data\csps.json" kapil@indev.eko.in:~/growth/

--- STEP 2. Log in to the server ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ssh -p 2222 kapil@indev.eko.in

--- STEP 3. On the SERVER - run it ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
bash ~/growth/deploy_growth.sh
# takes a few minutes (the rebuild installs pandas). It must end with:
#   DONE: Device Sales is live at https://indev.eko.in/csp-device-heartbeat/growth/
# if it prints STOP or WARNING, paste the whole output to Claude.

--- STEP 4. In the browser ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open the dashboard, press Ctrl+F5 once, click "Device Sales" in the sidebar. Check:
  - Overview shows 535 CSPs; Orders shows the 6 orders; Messages shows the past emails
  - the top-right pill says DRY RUN
  - https://indev.eko.in/csp-device-heartbeat/growth/order.html opens on a phone (mobile data, not office Wi-Fi)

--- STEP 5. Clean up the copies of CSP data ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
On the SERVER:   rm -f ~/growth/state.json ~/growth/csps.json ~/growth/extra.env ~/growth/growth_platform.tgz
On YOUR PC:      del growth_platform.tgz, extra.env   (in C:\Users\ASUS\Downloads\csp-device-monitor)

# restores app.py, base.html, hindi.py, requirements.txt and .env from the backup and rebuilds.
